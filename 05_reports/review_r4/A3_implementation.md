## Tier 2

### A3-T2-1. §3.5 "95% CI only marginally excludes 0.5" understates the data.
【Problem】 External AUC 0.638, 95% CI 0.532–0.748. The lower bound 0.532 is 0.032 above 0.5, so the CI *does* exclude 0.5. Calling it "only marginally excludes 0.5" is misleading — it implies the result is barely distinguishable from chance when the CI firmly excludes it.
【Evidence】 §3.5 l.109; `09_external_validation.csv` orientedSum_CI95_low=0.5317.
【Why it matters】 Inconsistent caution: the manuscript is simultaneously over-cautious here (understating a real exclusion) and over-confident elsewhere (false "nothing survives" in MR). Both should be aligned to the data.
【Specific fix】 "a separation whose 95% CI (0.532–0.748) excludes 0.5, though the point estimate is only 0.138 above chance."
