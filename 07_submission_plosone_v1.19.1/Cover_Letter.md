Dear PLOS ONE Editorial Team,

Please find enclosed our manuscript for consideration as an Original Research Article. This is a single-author, fully computational, auditable re-analysis of public sepsis transcriptomic cohorts (GSE65682, E-MTAB-4451) with a two-sample Mendelian randomisation layer.

We believe this work is a strong fit for PLOS ONE's soundness-first, novelty-agnostic editorial criteria:

- **Original methodological contribution.** We deliver a transparent, fully reproducible pipeline in which every reported number traces to a deposited result file (enforced by 32 automated audit assertions). The pipeline (i) confirms the immunosuppressed Mars1 endotype program, (ii) externally validates a 30-gene sepsis prognostic signature on an independent, cross-platform cohort (AUC 0.638, 95% CI 0.532–0.748), and (iii) provides an in-silico drug-repositioning shortlist together with an explicit experimental-validation blueprint.
- **Honest null results are reported as such.** The Mendelian-randomisation layer is uniformly null across the 15-test family (minimum q = 0.81) and is framed throughout as hypothesis-generating, not as causal support. We consider a rigorous, well-documented null a valid scientific contribution in its own right, and we present it transparently rather than overstating it.
- **Fully open data and code.** A versioned GitHub repository (tag v1.19.1) and a public Zenodo archive (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23042366) accompany the work. No new primary data were generated; all inputs are public.

We confirm that the manuscript (i) presents original research, (ii) describes methods in sufficient detail for reproduction, (iii) supports its conclusions with the data shown, and (iv) meets applicable ethics standards — the analysis uses public, de-identified data and was exempt from local IRB review (see the Ethics statement). Use of generative AI in manuscript preparation is disclosed in §2.12. We have no competing interests and no funding to declare.

We suggest handling areas: bioinformatics, immunology, critical care / sepsis, genomics.

Thank you for your consideration. We are happy to provide any additional materials needed for review.
